NSW Fuel Price Analysis & Prediction

Analysing a decade of NSW fuel prices using Python, statistical modelling and Power BI.

View Source Code on GitHub →

Project Overview

This project investigates historical petrol prices across New South Wales, using approximately ten years of fuel price data to explore pricing trends, market cycles and the relationship between fuel prices and broader economic factors.

The analysis combines historical E10 fuel prices with economic indicators, including crude oil prices, exchange rates, fuel excise and inflation, to investigate the factors influencing retail petrol prices.

Objectives

  • Explore historical NSW E10 petrol price movements.
  • Identify recurring fuel price cycles and peak-to-trough patterns.
  • Investigate the relationship between crude oil prices and retail fuel prices.
  • Account for exchange rates, fuel excise and inflation.
  • Develop a statistical regression model to investigate fuel price behaviour.

Tools & Technologies

Python Pandas NumPy Matplotlib Statsmodels Power BI DAX Regression Analysis

Statistical Modelling

The project also investigates the use of multiple linear regression to explain daily fuel price movements. The modelling process incorporates lagged fuel prices, crude oil prices converted into Australian dollars, daily fuel excise, inflation and a measure of time since the previous fuel price peak.

Python is used for data preparation, exploratory analysis, model estimation and residual diagnostics.
